# PSP settings

Settings uses Graphics, Controls, Gameplay, Audio and Languages. Graphics combines the earlier Graphics, Advanced and Effects categories. The content scrolls with D-pad focus, and both panels have matching heights. The client header, dropdown, checkbox, slider and selected-category art is used at PSP scale. See PSP_SETTINGS_MOVEMENT_FOLLOWUP.md for controls and the updated UI pack.

Ground decoration and its density/distance settings remain removed. Old grassdensity/groundclutter config keys have no effect. Legacy pack decoration is ignored, and the world exporter no longer generates it. Terrain textures, trees, buildings, fences, NPCs and collision retain their ordinary paths. See PSP_BRIDGE_MEMORY.md for that streaming fix.
